# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a tool for setting up developer machines, not a
place worth anyone having a bad day over.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Critique the code, the approach or the decision -- not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that people work on different platforms, at different levels of
  experience, and in different timezones.
- Say what you mean plainly. Directness is fine; contempt is not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing a review.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would read as intended to make someone feel
  unwelcome.

## Scope

Applies anywhere this project is represented -- issues, pull requests, commit
messages, and any space where you are speaking as a contributor to it.

## Reporting

Raise it privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/hyperi-io/hyperi-developer/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel available on this repository. Reports are handled
by the maintainers listed in [CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS).

We will look into anything reported, keep the reporter's identity to ourselves,
and act where action is warranted -- from a private word through to a ban from
the project's spaces.
